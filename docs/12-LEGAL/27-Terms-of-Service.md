## 1. Purpose
Closes the P18-02 finding: "No Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, DPA, or EULA" existed anywhere in the repository. This document is the ToS draft; Master Document 28 covers Privacy Policy; Master Document 29 covers the DPA.

## 2. Parties
This Agreement is between **DRC** ("Sonic", "we", "us") and the organization or individual registering a tenant account ("Customer", "you").

## 3. Service description
Sonic is a multi-tenant SaaS platform that ingests contact-centre conversation recordings/transcripts and produces compliance, risk, and agent-performance analytics via an AI-assisted processing pipeline (see Master Document 16 for the technical architecture referenced here at a customer-facing level).

## 4. Account & tenancy
- Customer registers under a unique subdomain (tenant); Customer is responsible for managing user access within its tenant (invitations, role assignment) via the Customer's own administrators.
- Sonic maintains platform-level administrative access solely for operations, support, and security purposes, isolated from tenant-scoped data access (see Master Document 15).

## 5. Acceptable use
Customer will not: upload content it does not have lawful rights to process; attempt to circumvent tenant isolation or rate limits; use the Service to violate applicable law; attempt prompt injection or adversarial manipulation of the AI pipeline (see Master Document 16, P7-09 red-team scope, which documents that Sonic actively tests for and defends against such attempts).

## 6. AI-generated output
Compliance, risk, and performance findings are generated by an AI-assisted pipeline combining deterministic rules and large-language-model analysis. Findings may include a confidence indicator and, per Sonic's internal policy (Master Document 16, P7-05), certain categories of significant findings require human review before being treated as final. **Customer acknowledges that AI-derived findings are decision-support, not a guaranteed determination of fact**, and Customer remains responsible for its own compliance and employment decisions.

## 7. Data processing & third parties
Sonic may process Customer's conversation content using a large-language-model provider (Anthropic) as described in the Privacy Policy (Master Document 28) and DPA (Master Document 29). Customer consents to this processing by using the Service, subject to the redaction and data-handling controls described there.

## 8. Billing
Usage-based pricing via AI credits, billed monthly, per the plan/tier selected at signup (see Master Document 10). Non-payment handling (dunning, suspension) follows the process specified in Master Document 6 §5A.

## 9. Data retention & deletion
Conversation content is retained per Sonic's data-retention policy (Master Document 18, P22-02) — currently a 30-day window from ingestion, subject to change with notice. Deletion requests are handled per the DSAR process (Master Document 18, P22-03).

## 10. Service levels
Target uptime and support commitments are set out in a separate SLA document (Master Document 6 references a 99.9% uptime target; a standalone SLA document remains **TO BE PRODUCED** — tracked as P15-03 in the Draft-2 remediation map, not yet authored as of this pass).
